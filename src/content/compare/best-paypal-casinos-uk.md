## Why PayPal beats cards for casino play

**Speed after approval.** Once a casino approves your withdrawal, PayPal transfers are near-instant. Debit card refunds ride the slower card network and typically take 1–3 working days to appear.

**No card details shared.** You log in to PayPal; the casino never sees your card number. If you play at several sites, that's several fewer places holding your payment data.

**One statement, easy limits.** All your gambling spend appears in one PayPal activity feed, which makes it much easier to track — and PayPal lets you remove a casino's billing agreement in two clicks.
