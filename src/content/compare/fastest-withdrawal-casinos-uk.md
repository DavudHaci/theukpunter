## The industry benchmark: what the regulator's data shows

The UKGC's market data makes the standard clear: **over 96% of withdrawals at licensed UK sites are processed automatically**, and only around 0.1% take more than 48 hours. Any operator that routinely takes days to approve a payout is choosing to — it is not an industry limitation.

## What actually determines payout speed

A casino withdrawal has two phases, and most players only think about the second:

1. **Internal approval** — the casino reviews and releases your request. This ranges from instant (automated) to 72 hours (manual, office-hours-only teams). This phase is where "slow casinos" lose.
2. **Payment network transfer** — once released: PayPal arrives in minutes, Visa Direct in under 2 hours where supported, standard card refunds in 1–3 working days.

## How to get paid faster (at any casino)

- **Verify before you deposit.** Upload ID and proof of address on day one. Verification-after-withdrawal is the #1 cause of multi-day delays.
- **Use an e-wallet.** [PayPal casinos](/best-paypal-casinos-uk/) consistently offer the shortest after-approval times.
- **Withdraw to the deposit method.** Anti-money-laundering rules force casinos to return funds to the source first; fighting this adds manual review time.
- **Avoid weekend requests at office-hours operators.** A Friday 6pm withdrawal at a manual-review casino may sit until Monday.
- **Don't reverse withdrawals.** Beyond being a known problem-gambling pattern, cancelling and re-requesting resets the queue.

## Red flags for slow payers

- Withdrawal "pending periods" of 48 hours or more stated in the terms
- Low weekly withdrawal caps (e.g. £500/week) that drip-feed large wins
- Document requests repeated at every withdrawal, not just the first
- No published processing times at all
