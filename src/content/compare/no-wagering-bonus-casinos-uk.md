## The 2026 rule change that reshaped UK bonuses

From **January 2026**, the UKGC capped wagering requirements at **10x** and banned mixing wagering across products in a single promotion. The era of the 50x bonus is over at licensed sites. That's made the market split into two camps:

1. **10x wagering offers** — bigger headline amounts, still a meaningful hurdle (£100 bonus = £1,000 in bets).
2. **True no-wagering offers** — smaller amounts, zero strings on winnings.

Anything advertising 35x or 50x wagering to UK players today is either out of date or **not UKGC-licensed** — treat it as a red flag and check the licence with our [two-minute UKGC guide](/guides/how-to-check-ukgc-licence/).

## How to compare offers like an analyst

The real value of a bonus is roughly: *expected winnings × probability you can actually withdraw them*. In practice:

- **No wagering, 50 spins at 10p** = £5 of spins; whatever they win (typically £2–£8 on a 96% RTP slot) is cash. Real value: a few pounds, *guaranteed accessible*.
- **10x wagering, £50 bonus** = you must stake £500. On a 96% RTP slot the expected cost of that wagering is around £20 — often more than the bonus is worth by the time you finish.

## Fine print checklist

- **Winnings as cash, not bonus funds** — the defining test of "no wagering"
- **Spin value** — 50 spins at 10p (£5) vs 20 spins at 50p (£10): count the total, not the spins
- **Eligible games** — usually one featured slot; check its RTP variant
- **Max win caps** — rare on no-wagering offers but worth confirming
- **Expiry** — typically 3–7 days to use the spins
