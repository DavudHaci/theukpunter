Wagering requirements are how a "£50 free" bonus can end up worth nothing — and occasionally how it can be genuinely valuable. The difference is arithmetic that operators hope you won't do. Let's do it.

## The definition, precisely

A wagering (or playthrough) requirement is the total amount you must **stake** before bonus winnings convert to withdrawable cash.

> **£20 bonus, 10x wagering = £200 in bets before you can cash out bonus winnings.**

Watch the multiplier base: "10x bonus" on a £20 bonus is £200, but "10x deposit + bonus" on a £20 deposit + £20 bonus is £400 — twice the hurdle from the same headline number. Since the UKGC's January 2026 rules, **10x is the legal maximum** and deposit-plus-bonus structures have been largely squeezed out — any site offering 35x+ to UK players today is worth [a licence check](/guides/how-to-check-ukgc-licence/).

## What wagering actually costs: the expected-value formula

Every bet you place while clearing wagering pays the house edge. So:

> **Expected cost of wagering ≈ wagering amount × house edge**

Worked examples for a £50 bonus at 10x (£500 wagering):

| Game used | RTP | Expected cost of £500 wagering | Bonus worth (expected) |
|---|---|---|---|
| High RTP slot | 97% | £15 | +£35 |
| Average slot | 96% | £20 | +£30 |
| Low-variant slot | 92% | £40 | +£10 |

The same bonus is worth three times more cleared on a 97% game than a 92% one — which is why our [high RTP slots list](/high-rtp-slots-uk/) matters most to bonus players, and why you should always [check the RTP variant](/guides/what-is-rtp-slots-explained/) at your specific casino.

**The honest caveat:** these are averages. Clearing £500 of wagering on a volatile slot with a £50 starting balance busts more often than the expected value suggests. Positive EV is not a guarantee — treat bonuses as discounted entertainment, not income.

## The four traps in the fine print

1. **Game contribution rates.** Slots count 100%; roulette, blackjack and live games often count 10–20% or nothing. £100 on blackjack may clear £10 of wagering.
2. **Maximum bet while wagering.** Usually £2–£5 per spin. Exceed it — even accidentally — and the casino may void the bonus and winnings. This is the most enforced term in the book.
3. **Expiry windows.** Commonly 7–30 days. Unfinished wagering at expiry forfeits the bonus and its winnings.
4. **Excluded games and max win caps.** Jackpot slots are usually excluded; some offers cap convertible winnings (e.g. £100 max), which slashes the real value.

## The alternative: skip wagering entirely

The cleanest solution to wagering maths is a bonus with none. **No-wagering offers** pay winnings as immediately withdrawable cash — smaller headline numbers, dramatically higher real value for most players. - Read the contribution table before your first spin
