The honest answer: anywhere from **20 minutes to 5 working days** — and the difference is almost entirely down to which casino you chose and whether you verified your account in advance.

Here's the full breakdown of where the time goes and how to shrink it.

## Withdrawal times by payment method

| Method | After casino approval | Typical total (well-run casino) |
|---|---|---|
| PayPal / e-wallets | Minutes | 2–12 hours |
| Visa Direct (fast debit payout) | Under 2 hours | 3–24 hours |

The pattern is clear: the **payment network is rarely the bottleneck**. The casino's internal approval process is.

## The two-phase reality of every withdrawal

**Phase 1 — casino approval.** Your request sits in the operator's queue until it's released. At modern operators this is automated and instant: the UKGC's own market data shows **over 96% of withdrawals at licensed UK sites are processed automatically**, and only around 0.1% take more than 48 hours. At laggards, a human reviews your request during office hours.

**Phase 2 — the money moves.** E-wallets are near-instant. Visa Direct has brought many debit card payouts under 2 hours. Standard card refunds ride the slow settlement rails: 1–3 working days, no matter how good the casino is.

## The five causes of delayed withdrawals

1. **Verification after withdrawal.** The single biggest cause of multi-day delays. UKGC casinos must verify your identity before paying out; if you skip document upload at signup, your first withdrawal triggers it.
2. **Manual review thresholds.** Many operators auto-approve small amounts but manually review anything above, say, £500 — or any first withdrawal.
3. **Office-hours payment teams.** A withdrawal requested Friday night can sit until Monday morning.
4. **Bonus term checks.** If you played with a bonus, the casino may verify wagering was completed correctly — see [how wagering requirements work](/guides/casino-wagering-requirements-explained/).
5. **Pending periods.** Some terms include a 24–48 hour "processing window". This is a policy choice, and the best operators have abolished it.

At a UKGC-licensed casino you have real protections. The operator must tell you why a withdrawal is held. If you dispute the outcome, every licensed casino must offer **free alternative dispute resolution** (typically IBAS or eCOGRA), and their decisions bind the operator.
