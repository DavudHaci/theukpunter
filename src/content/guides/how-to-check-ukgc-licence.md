Every legitimate online casino serving Great Britain must hold a licence from the **UK Gambling Commission**. Checking takes two minutes, requires no account, and is the single most valuable habit a UK player can build — because every other protection flows from it.

## The two-minute check

1. **Scroll to the casino's footer.** Find the operating company name and licence number — e.g. "Operated by Example Ltd, licensed and regulated by the Gambling Commission (licence 000-000000-R-000000-000)".
2. **Open the public register.** Go to the Gambling Commission's website (gamblingcommission.gov.uk) and open the **Public register of licence holders**.
3. **Search the company name** (not the casino brand — one company often runs several brands).
4. **Verify three things:**
   - Status is **Active** (not surrendered, suspended or revoked)
   - Licensed activities include **"Casino — R" (remote)**
   - The **domain you're playing on** appears in the account's listed domains — this is the step that catches clone sites
5. **Cross-check the licence number** matches the footer.

If any step fails, don't deposit. It's that simple.

## Red flags that skip the register entirely

- **No company name or licence number in the footer** — licensed operators are required to display this
- **"Licensed by Curaçao/Anjouan" targeting UK players** — not valid for Great Britain
- **A UKGC logo that isn't a link** or links to the regulator's homepage rather than a register entry — logos are trivially faked; the register is not
- **35x+ bonus wagering** — the UKGC capped wagering at 10x in January 2026, so oversized requirements signal an unlicensed operator ([why that matters](/guides/casino-wagering-requirements-explained/))
- **Stakes above £5 per spin on slots** — UK-licensed sites are capped ([stake limits explained](/guides/uk-slot-stake-limits-explained/))
- **Credit card deposits accepted** — banned at UK-licensed operators since 2020

## What the licence actually buys you

| Protection | UKGC-licensed | Unlicensed |
|---|---|---|
| Player funds segregated from company money | Yes (disclosed level) | No |
| Certified game fairness / RTP | Yes | Unverifiable |
| Free binding dispute resolution (IBAS, eCOGRA) | Yes | None |
| GamStop self-exclusion applies | Yes | No |
| Regulator that fines and revokes | Yes | No |
| Realistic chance of recovering a withheld withdrawal | High | Poor |

This is why "the withdrawal test" and licensing go together: at a licensed casino, a stalled payout has an escalation path that ends with a binding decision ([how withdrawals work](/guides/how-long-do-casino-withdrawals-take-uk/)). At an unlicensed one, a stalled payout usually ends with a closed live-chat window.
