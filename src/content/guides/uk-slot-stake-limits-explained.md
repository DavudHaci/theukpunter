Since 2025, every online slot at a UK-licensed casino has a hard stake cap: **£5 per spin if you're 25 or over, £2 per spin if you're 18–24**. Combined with earlier rules on spin speed and autoplay, these are the biggest changes to UK online slots since regulation began. Here's exactly what the rules say and what they mean in practice.

## The rules at a glance

| Rule | Detail | In force since |
|---|---|---|
| £5 max stake per spin | Players aged 25+ | 9 April 2025 |
| £2 max stake per spin | Players aged 18–24 | 21 May 2025 |
| Minimum 2.5s spin speed | Every slot spin must take at least 2.5 seconds | October 2021 |
| Autoplay banned | You must press for every spin | October 2021 |
| Losses-disguised-as-wins banned | No celebration effects for wins below your stake | October 2021 |
| Bonus wagering capped at 10x | Applies to all promotions | January 2026 |

These apply to **online slots only** — live dealer and table games are not stake-capped (operators set their own table limits — see our [live casino comparison](/live-dealer-casinos-uk/)).

## What it means in practice

**For most players: very little.** Industry data consistently showed the overwhelming majority of slot spins were already below £2. If you typically stake 20p–£1, nothing changes.

**For high-stakes players: a hard ceiling.** There is no VIP exemption, no verification level that unlocks higher stakes at a licensed site. £5 (or £2) is the maximum, full stop.

**For session pace:** with a 2.5-second minimum spin and no autoplay, a maximum-speed session is 24 spins per minute. At the £5 cap, the theoretical maximum stake-through is £7,200/hour — sounds enormous, but it's a fraction of what pre-2021 turbo autoplay allowed.

## The honest trade-off: offshore sites

Searches for casinos "without limits" have grown since the caps arrived, so let's address it directly. Unlicensed offshore sites do offer uncapped stakes, turbo spins and autoplay. What you give up by playing there:

- **Fund protection** — no requirement to segregate player balances; if the operator folds, your balance goes with it
- **Dispute resolution** — no IBAS/eCOGRA; a confiscated withdrawal is simply gone
- **Fairness auditing** — no UKGC-mandated RTP certification ([what RTP means](/guides/what-is-rtp-slots-explained/))
- **GamStop** — self-exclusion doesn't apply, which is precisely why players in recovery are targeted by these sites
- **Legal standing** — gambling debts and disputes with unlicensed operators have no meaningful enforcement path
